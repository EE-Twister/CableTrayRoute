Cyber Compliance

Design-stage evidence screening for NERC CIP and IEC 62443 cyber-physical controls.

Scope and limitations

This study identifies missing asset evidence for CIP-002, CIP-005, CIP-007, and IEC 62443-3-3 SR 1/3/5. It is not a compliance certification; asset owners must determine formal applicability and accept evidence.

Cyber assets and evidence

Seed candidate assets from the equipment, panel, and one-line records, then complete zone, protocol, remote-access, firmware, and evidence fields.
