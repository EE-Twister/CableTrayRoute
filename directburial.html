Direct Burial Circuit Sizing

Find how many circuits of a given current can be direct buried side by side at a chosen cover depth, and which conductor size and circuit spacing gives the lowest installed cost per foot.

About This Study

Each cable is modelled with the IEC 60287 thermal circuit. Mutual heating from every neighbouring cable is added to the external thermal resistance with the image method, and each circuit is rated at its hottest position (a centre circuit of the row). Costs combine the cable price allowances from the Cost Estimate page with an editable trench excavation and backfill allowance, so the result is a screening comparison, not a quotation.

This does not replace NEC 310.14 / Annex B or a full Neher-McGrath study. Confirm the final design against the governing code tables.
