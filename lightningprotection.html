Method & Assumptions

This study provides a screening-level lightning protection assessment informed by IEC 62305:2024 and IEEE 998-2026. Medium- and high-voltage arrester screening is limited to systems above 1 kV; lower-voltage systems are routed to an SPD review.

Risk:

NSG = 0.04 · Td^1.25                 (legacy estimate, strike points/km²/yr)
Ad = Af + (3H)P + π·(3H)²             (collection area, m²)
Nd = NSG · Ad · Cd · 1e-6            (direct strikes/yr)
E  = 1 − Nc/Nd  →  LPL I / II / III / IV

Footprints: Af is plan area and P is plan perimeter. Rectangular and circular modes calculate both values from dimensions; custom mode uses entered area and perimeter. The coverage view assumes a centered mast and compares its radius with the farthest protected point.

The required Lightning Protection Level (LPL) is the least-restrictive class whose interception capability meets E: IV=0.84, III=0.91, II=0.97, I=0.99. Requirements above 0.99 need additional risk-reduction measures.

Rolling sphere (electrogeometric model): each LPL has a sphere radius R (I=20 m, II=30 m, III=45 m, IV=60 m). A single mast of height h protects an object of height hx out to a radius:

rp = √(h(2R − h)) − √(hx(2R − hx))

Surge arrester: for systems above 1 kV only, minimum MCOV Uc ≥ 1.05·VLL/√3 (effectively grounded) or 1.05·VLL (ungrounded); rated voltage ≈ MCOV/0.8. Final selection must include temporary overvoltage and insulation-coordination checks.

References: IEC 62305-1/-2/-3:2024; IEEE Std 998-2026; IEEE C62.22; IEC 60099-5:2018.

Lightning & Surge Protection Help

This page screens direct-strike frequency, visualizes a structure and single-air-terminal concept, and identifies the applicable surge-protection workflow. It is not a final lightning-protection design.

When to use

  • Utility substation direct-stroke shielding concepts informed by IEEE 998-2026.
  • Structural lightning-protection screening informed by IEC 62305:2024.
  • Preliminary arrester-voltage screening for systems above 1 kV.

Inputs

  • Lightning density — prefer a mapped ground strike-point density; thunderstorm days provide only a legacy screening estimate.
  • Location factor — accounts for nearby taller objects that intercept strikes.
  • Footprint shape — rectangular and circular modes calculate plan area and perimeter; custom mode accepts area, perimeter, and the farthest protected point.
  • Air-terminal tip height — is the actual elevation used for the single-mast coverage calculation.
  • System voltage / grounding — selects either a low-voltage SPD review or a preliminary arrester screen above 1 kV.

Interpreting results

  • Required LPL — the least-restrictive class whose nominal interception capability meets the calculated screening efficiency.
  • Plan coverage — compares the rolling-sphere coverage radius with the farthest protected point from the assumed centered mast.
  • Elevation coverage — shows true circular rolling-sphere arcs with equal horizontal and vertical scales.
  • Down-conductors — preliminary count and minimum cross-section when an LPS is indicated.
  • Surge protection — low-voltage SPD review at or below 1 kV; preliminary MCOV and duty-cycle rating above 1 kV.

Limitations

  • Screening-level — a simplified direct-strike frequency comparison, not the full IEC 62305-2:2024 risk assessment.
  • Single-mast protection only; multi-mast and shield-wire layouts need a dedicated study.
  • Verify temporary-overvoltage duty, insulation coordination, grounding, and a complete risk assessment before final design.
